Scoped to a repo. Or scoped to you.

In the editor

Claude Code . Codex . Cursor

  • Lives in a working directory
  • Starts when you open the terminal
  • Answers where you asked
  • Ends when the session ends

  • Lives on a machine that is yours
  • Runs whether or not you are there
  • Answers on your phone
  • Carries on next week

05 . build

Before you leave it running

  • SilenceOnly tell me when something changed. A job that reports every hour is a job I will mute.
  • StateIt has to remember what it saw last time. That is your job, not the scheduler's.
  • FailureA failed check is not a no. Never let an error overwrite the last good answer.
  • DuplicatesDelivery is at least once. Expect the occasional repeat and design for it.
  • SleepA closed laptop runs nothing. That is the whole case for a small server.
